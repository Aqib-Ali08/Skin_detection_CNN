How It Works

Welcome To

The Skin Diagonesis

Upload Your Skin Image

Start by uploading a clear, high-quality image of the skin area you’re concerned about. Our tool is designed to analyze various aspects of the skin condition, so ensure that the image is well-lit and in focus for the most accurate results.

Analyze the Image

Once your image is uploaded, our advanced algorithms and machine learning technology analyze the skin features and patterns. This process involves evaluating the texture, color, and shape of the skin to detect any abnormalities that might indicate a risk of skin cancer.

Receive Your Risk Assessment

After the analysis, you will receive a detailed risk assessment report. This report provides an overview of your potential risk level and highlights any areas that may require further attention. The results are presented in a clear and easy-to-understand format.

Take Action

Based on your risk assessment, we provide recommendations for next steps. If your results suggest a higher risk, we encourage you to consult a healthcare professional for a comprehensive evaluation. Additionally, explore our resources for tips on skin health and preventive measures.
